## About GarageOS

GarageOS is an independent automotive technology ecosystem dedicated to developing intelligent, connected and reliable software solutions for automotive professionals worldwide.

We combine hands-on automotive engineering experience with modern software development to simplify complex technical workflows and empower independent automotive businesses.

## Our Mission

To make professional automotive technology more accessible, efficient and connected through continuous innovation and intelligent software engineering.
